Our story

A small kitchen with a big appetite

Red Chilly started in August 2024 when Pratap Singh Malik set up a cloud kitchen in Ganga Nagar, Meerut, with one idea: quick, tasty veg food at prices that make ordering in an easy habit.

We have no dine-in counter. Everything we cook goes straight into your delivery bag, so the whole kitchen stays focused on one thing, your food.
